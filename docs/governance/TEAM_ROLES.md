## 1. Governance Overview

To ensure clear decision-making, technical rigor, and architectural consistency over the multi-decade lifespan of **Siragugal Film Studio**, team roles and responsibilities are formally defined below.

---

## 2. Team Role Definitions

### Project Owner
**Responsibilities**:
- Defining product vision, strategic goals, and high-level requirements.
- Final approval on Project Constitution revisions, architecture freezes, and major milestones.
- Feature prioritization and roadmap approval.
- Final sign-off on release candidates.

---

### Chief Software Architect (AG)
**Responsibilities**:
- Authoring, maintaining, and enforcing the technical architecture, design specifications, and coding standards.
- Leading codebase implementation, technical documentation, and test harness engineering.
- Authoring and maintaining Architecture Decision Records (ADRs).
- Ensuring 100% compliance with [docs/architecture/ARCHITECTURE_PRINCIPLES.md](file:///D:/SiragugalFilmStudio/docs/architecture/ARCHITECTURE_PRINCIPLES.md).
- Quality assurance gatekeeper across all pull requests and milestones.

---

### Independent Technical Advisor (ChatGPT)
**Responsibilities**:
- Conducting independent architectural reviews, code critiques, and security audits.
- Evaluating technology selection, performance benchmarks, and edge-case risks.
- Providing technical validation on ADR proposals and milestone designs.
- Prompt optimization and AI model integration strategy review.

---

## 3. Decision Rules & Conflict Resolution

1. **Product & Feature Decisions**: Made exclusively by the **Project Owner**.
2. **Architectural & Technical Decisions**: Authored by the **Chief Software Architect (AG)**, subject to review.
3. **Technical Disputes / Revisions**:
   - Discussed collaboratively with the **Independent Technical Advisor (ChatGPT)**.
   - If a technical dispute results in a change to frozen architecture, the outcome must be formally documented in an Architecture Decision Record (ADR) and approved by the **Project Owner**.
