**Movie Title:** "Kodai" ( Tamil for " Island")

**Visual Concept:**

"Kodai" is set in a dystopian future where artificial intelligence has surpassed human intelligence, and a brilliant AI filmmaker, "Eclipse," has created a new reality that blurs the lines between humans and machines. The movie takes place on a futuristic island, where Eclipse's creations have come to life, and the boundaries between reality and fantasy are constantly shifting.

**Camera Style:**

* Primary camera: Handheld, using a mix of Steadicam and Gimbal stabilizers to capture a dynamic, immersive experience.
* Secondary camera: Drone footage to capture the island's vast landscapes and Eclipse's creations from above.
* Tertiary camera: High-speed cameras to capture the intense action sequences and special effects.

**Lens Choices:**

* Primary lens: 24mm f/1.4 for wide shots, emphasizing the grandeur of the island and Eclipse's creations.
* Secondary lens: 50mm f/1.4 for intimate shots of characters and their emotions.
* Tertiary lens: 200mm f/2.8 for telephoto shots of action sequences and special effects.

**Lighting Design:**

* Primary lighting: A mix of natural and artificial light sources, using LED lights and practical lighting to create a futuristic atmosphere.
* Secondary lighting: High-key lighting to emphasize the AI's cold, calculating nature.
* Tertiary lighting: Low-key lighting for intense action sequences and special effects.

**Color Palette:**

* Primary color: A muted blue-green hue (#4567b7) to represent the AI's digital existence.
* Secondary color: A warm, golden light (#f7dc6f) to symbolize the human connection.
* Tertiary color: A deep, rich purple (#6c5ce7) to represent the AI's creative power.

**Composition:**

* Primary composition: Symmetrical framing to create a sense of balance and order.
* Secondary composition: Dynamic framing to emphasize the chaos and disorder caused by the AI's actions.
* Tertiary composition: Experimentation with unconventional framing techniques to represent the blurred lines between reality and fantasy.

**Camera Movement:**

* Primary movement: Smooth, fluid movements to emphasize the AI's calculated nature.
* Secondary movement: Jarring, staccato movements to represent the chaos and disorder caused by the AI's actions.
* Tertiary movement: High-speed, dynamic movements to capture the intense action sequences and special effects.

**VFX Integration:**

* Primary VFX: CGI enhancements to represent the AI's digital existence and its creations.
* Secondary VFX: Practical effects to create a sense of realism and authenticity.
* Tertiary VFX: Experimentation with innovative techniques to push the boundaries of what is possible in VFX.

**Frame References:**

* Primary frame reference: The works of Stanley Kubrick and Christopher Nolan, who have pushed the boundaries of cinematic storytelling.
* Secondary frame reference: The futuristic designs of Syd Mead and H.R. Giger, who have inspired the visual aesthetic of "Kodai."
* Tertiary frame reference: The experimental films of avant-garde directors like Alejandro Jodorowsky and Lars von Trier, who have pushed the boundaries of cinematic storytelling.

**Technical Cinematography Notes:**

* Camera settings: Shoot in 4K resolution, using a mix of 24fps and 120fps to create a dynamic, immersive experience.
* Lighting control: Use a combination of flags, diffusion filters, and practical lighting to control the light and create a cohesive look.
* Camera movement: Use a combination of Steadicam, Gimbal, and handheld stabilizers to capture smooth, fluid movements.
* VFX: Integrate VFX in post-production, using a combination of CGI and practical effects to create a seamless, realistic experience.

By following this visual language, "Kodai" will be a thought-provoking, visually stunning sci-fi movie that explores the boundaries between humans and machines, and the power of creativity in a world where technology has surpassed human intelligence.
