**Movie Title:** "Mindframe" (மண்டலம்)

**Genre:** Tamil Sci-Fi, Action

**Logline:** When a brilliant AI director is created to revolutionize the film industry, she must navigate the blurred lines between reality and virtual reality to uncover a sinister plot to control the minds of the world's population.

	+ Marketing: ₹30 crores

* Medium-scale production with a mix of practical and CGI effects
* Shooting locations: Chennai, Hyderabad, and Mumbai

**Shooting Schedule:**

* Principal photography: 30 days
